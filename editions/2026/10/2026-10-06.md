### 🔹 Story 5: [Apple to Tighten Full Disk Access Controls in macOS Amid AI Risks](https://www.securityweek.com/apple-to-tighten-full-disk-access-controls-in-macos-amid-ai-risks/)
> **Category:** 🛡️ Cybersecurity & Breaches | **Source:** SecurityWeek

- 📌 **What happened?**: Apple has announced tightened Full Disk Access controls in macOS in light of the risks posed by continuously evolving AI agents.

- 🔍 **Key details**: Apple introduced Full Disk Access in macOS Mojave (10.14) to provide users with control over applications’ access to the entire system. The feature can be toggled on or off from Apple menu > System Settings > Privacy & Security.

- 💡 **Context & Impact**: By default, macOS restricts applications from accessing system-protected areas and sensitive user data. Granting an application Full Disk Access waives these protections, allowing that software to read or modify private files, including a user’s mail, messages, and browsing history.
